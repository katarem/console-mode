Button icons: «Input Prompts» by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
Xbox Series, PlayStation Series and Nintendo Switch sets.
License: Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The «*.light.png» files are the same icons with their white parts darkened so that they can be seen on light
backgrounds; the «*.dark.png» files are the original ones. Each file is named after the XInput button whose physical
position it shows (for example Nintendo.A is Nintendo's «B» button, the bottom one).
